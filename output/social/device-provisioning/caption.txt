Choosing a device is a security decision — not just a price comparison.

The Device Provisioning Toolkit brings device discovery, security assessment and procurement context into one workflow. It helps people compare personal, business and government-use devices, then gives them practical guidance for setup, hardening and recovery.

I built it from the question: what should we provide, and how do we decide safely?

This is one example of how my research becomes something people can use.
