# Student Finance England — UCL MSc route

## Current position

For a new full-time UCL MSc beginning in September 2026, the relevant route is the **Postgraduate Master’s Loan**, not undergraduate Student Finance. Student Finance England says applications for 2026/27 are open and the maximum loan is **£13,206** for the whole Master's course. The loan is paid to the student in three instalments and can be used towards tuition and living costs.

The GOV.UK eligibility page explicitly says that a previous **postgraduate diploma or certificate** does not by itself prevent eligibility. It also says previous Master’s-level funding, an existing Master’s/equivalent qualification, age, nationality/residency and repayment status can affect the decision. This is why the application must describe the Kingston award accurately as a PGDip, not an MSc.

## UCL cost picture

- Published UK tuition for Information Security MSc (2026/27): **£21,500**.
- Maximum Postgraduate Master’s Loan: **£13,206**.
- Indicative tuition gap before living costs: **£8,294**.
- UCL separately states that successful UK postgraduate-taught offer holders normally pay a 2.5% fee deposit, subject to the offer and current policy.

The loan does not guarantee an offer, and an offer does not guarantee funding. Re-check both figures in the application portals before committing to any payment.

## Application route

1. Apply for the UCL MSc first or keep the application in progress until the course is confirmed.
2. Open the official GOV.UK **Student Finance England postgraduate application** route.
3. Select the 2026/27 Postgraduate Master’s Loan for a full-time taught Master’s course.
4. Enter the UCL course and start-date details exactly as shown on the offer/application record.
5. In previous study, state the Kingston award as: **Postgraduate Diploma in Network & Information Security, Distinction, Kingston University London, awarded 23 March 2026**.
6. Keep the UCL offer/application reference and the SFE Customer Reference Number together.
7. Submit only after checking identity, residency, previous-study and bank details on the final review screen.

## Do not do this

Do not describe the Kingston route as a completed MSc or apply for funding for a single dissertation module. UCL has indicated that its course is a fresh full-MSc application, and Student Finance England needs the qualification and course structure to match the formal records.
