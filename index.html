About Me

I'm Nathan Brown-Bennett, a creative technologist with a Postgraduate Diploma in Network and Information Security, awarded with Distinction by Kingston University London. I research how people and organisations make safer technology decisions, then communicate those findings through useful systems, clear writing and creative work.

My practice sits between cybersecurity, software, education and performance. I am currently preparing to progress from the PGDip into the MSc while developing BstudioB as the company home for products and client-facing work.

How do we decide what devices we provide?

My 2024 BSc dissertation examined how governments, schools, employers and consumers choose devices when security, practicality and reliability evidence is fragmented. It developed a provisioning framework and prototype that has since become a working Device Provisioning Toolkit.
